Join the group

Build a picture of galaxy assembly with us

I welcome conversations with curious researchers at different career stages. Potential projects span measuring chemical abundances of resolved stars in nearby galaxies, modeling the mass distributions of local dwarf galaxies, and working with large survey data to explore galaxy evolution.

Get in touch

I am currently seeking students and postdocs to join my group. If you are interested in working together, please send me your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Graduate study

Graduate students

I encourage current graduate students to reach out to discuss potential projects and fellowship opportunities through ANID (deadline typically in November). I also welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students on our program (deadline usually in July).

Postdoctoral research

Postdoctoral positions

Researchers with experience or interest in galactic archaeology, stellar populations, resolved star spectroscopy and photometry, or Local Group survey science are welcome to inquire about applying for fellowship opportunities, including the Fondecyt Postdoctoral Fellowship (for Chilean nationals with awarded PhDs, deadline in September) and ESO Comité Mixto postdoctoral funds (deadline typically in July).

Undergraduate research

Undergraduate students

Currently mentored students:
